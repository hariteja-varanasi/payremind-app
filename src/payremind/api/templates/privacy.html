Privacy policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

Who we are

PayRemind ("we") helps Indian businesses ("merchants") remind their customers about unpaid invoices over WhatsApp. Contact and grievance officer: [name, email, postal address].

Our two roles

  • For merchants' own account data (owner phone number, business name, GSTIN, billing address, subscription records) we are the data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).
  • For the merchant's customers' data that a merchant uploads (customer names, phone numbers, invoice details, promises to pay) the merchant is the data fiduciary and we act as their data processor, only on their instructions, under our Data Processing Agreement.

What we collect and why

  • Owner phone number: to sign you in with a one-time code sent on WhatsApp.
  • Invoice files you upload: to show overdue invoices and send reminders you set up.
  • Billing details and payment references: to charge your subscription and issue GST invoices. Card and UPI details are handled by Razorpay; we never see or store them.
  • Opt-out records (phone numbers that replied STOP): to stop sending reminders to them.
  • Security logs (IP address, time, action): to protect the service and meet CERT-In log retention rules.

Money

Payments from your customers go directly to your own UPI ID. We never receive, hold or move your customers' money.

Who we share data with

Only the service providers needed to run PayRemind: Amazon Web Services (hosting, Mumbai region, India), Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business messaging), and Razorpay (subscription payments). We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Where data is stored

In India (AWS Mumbai region). WhatsApp messages pass through Meta's systems, which may be outside India.

How long we keep it

  • Invoice and customer data: while your account is active, deleted within 30 days after you close it.
  • Billing records and GST invoices: as long as tax law requires (currently at least 8 years in our understanding; to be confirmed).
  • Security logs: 180 days, as required by CERT-In directions.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance with our grievance officer. If we don't resolve it, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India. Customers of a merchant should contact that merchant first; we will help the merchant respond.

Security

Encryption in transit and at rest, access limited to people who need it, hashed sign-in codes and session tokens, and incident reporting to CERT-In within 6 hours and to affected users and the Data Protection Board as the DPDP Rules require.

Children

PayRemind is for businesses. We don't knowingly process children's data.

Changes

We'll tell merchants about material changes by WhatsApp or email before they take effect.
