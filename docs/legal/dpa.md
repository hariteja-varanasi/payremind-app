# Data Processing Agreement (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT, not reviewed by a lawyer.** Have a qualified professional review this before any
> merchant signs it. Bracketed items must be filled in.

Between **[Merchant legal name]** ("Merchant", data fiduciary) and **[PayRemind legal entity]**
("PayRemind", data processor). It forms part of the PayRemind Terms of Service.

## 1. Subject and purpose
PayRemind processes the personal data the Merchant uploads (customer names, phone numbers,
invoice numbers, amounts, due dates, promises to pay, opt-out replies) only to show the
Merchant's receivables and send WhatsApp payment reminders the Merchant configures.

## 2. Instructions
PayRemind processes this data only on the Merchant's documented instructions (the settings and
actions in the product) and as required by Indian law. PayRemind will tell the Merchant if it
believes an instruction breaks the DPDP Act, 2023 or the DPDP Rules, 2025.

## 3. Merchant's duties
The Merchant confirms it has a lawful basis under the DPDP Act to use each customer's data for
payment reminders, gives customers any required notice, and handles their rights requests.

## 4. Confidentiality and staff
Only PayRemind staff who need access have it, and they are bound by confidentiality.

## 5. Security
PayRemind keeps reasonable security safeguards, including: encryption in transit (TLS) and at
rest (AWS KMS); tenant isolation tested on every release; hashed sign-in codes and session
tokens; access logging kept for 180 days; backups with tested restores; and least-privilege
access to production.

## 6. Sub-processors
Approved: Amazon Web Services India (hosting, ap-south-1 Mumbai); Meta Platforms (WhatsApp
Business Platform); Razorpay (only for the Merchant's own subscription, not customer data).
PayRemind gives 30 days' notice before adding or replacing a sub-processor that handles
customer data, and the Merchant may object and terminate.

## 7. Personal data breaches
PayRemind notifies the Merchant without undue delay, and in any case within **[24]** hours of
becoming aware of a breach affecting the Merchant's data, with the details the Merchant needs to
meet its own DPDP and CERT-In (6-hour) reporting duties. PayRemind reports to CERT-In itself
where the directions require.

## 8. Assistance
PayRemind helps the Merchant answer customers' access, correction and erasure requests and
grievances, using the product's export and delete functions where possible.

## 9. Location
Data is stored in India. WhatsApp message content passes through Meta's infrastructure.

## 10. Return and deletion
When the agreement ends, PayRemind deletes the Merchant's customer data within 30 days, unless
law requires it to be kept, and confirms deletion in writing on request.

## 11. Audit
On reasonable notice, at most once a year, PayRemind provides information needed to show
compliance with this agreement.
